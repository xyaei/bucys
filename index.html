What we do

  • Workshops

    Hands-on sessions where we try security tools and learn together. All levels are welcome.

  • CTFs

    Capture-the-flag events. Compete on your own or with a team, from a first challenge to a full competition.

  • Speakers

    Industry guests who talk about the work they do and how they got into the field.

  • Networking

    Meet other students and people with similar interests, and people in the industry.

  • Projects

    Hackathons and builds we take on together, from a small project to a team project.

  • Discussion

    A place for talks about cybersecurity, the latest news, and questions people bring.

Areas

Security covers a lot of ground. These are some of the paths members look into.

  • Red team

    Thinking like an attacker, and looking for weak spots in a system you are allowed to test.

  • Blue team

    Defending: watching for trouble, responding when something breaks, and tightening what you run.

  • Network security

    How traffic moves, and how to keep it from being read or sent somewhere it should not go.

  • Cloud security

    Accounts, storage, and services that live with a cloud provider instead of in one room.

  • Application security

    How websites and apps fail, and how to build them so those failures are harder.

  • Digital forensics

    Piecing together what happened after an incident, from logs and files left behind.
